The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has updated its July 2026 World Economic Outlook, projecting global headline inflation to increase from 4.1% in 2025 to 4.7% in 2026, before declining to 3.9% in 2027. These upwardly revised projections indicate that the disinflation trend observed since early 2024 has stalled. The IMF noted that risks to the outlook are more balanced but still tilted to the downside, with potential renewed Middle East conflicts and trade fragmentation posing threats to commodity prices, supply chains, and overall financial conditions.

总结：国际货币基金组织（IMF）在2026年7月的《世界经济展望》更新中，上调了全球总体通胀预期，预计将从2025年的4.1%升至2026年的4.7%，随后在2027年降至3.9%。 这些上调的预测表明，自2024年初以来的通胀下降趋势已停滞。 IMF指出，展望风险虽然更趋平衡，但仍偏向下行，中东冲突重燃和贸易碎片化可能对大宗商品价格、供应链和金融状况构成威胁。
