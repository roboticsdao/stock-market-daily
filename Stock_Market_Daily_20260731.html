US Economy — 美国第二季度GDP增长放缓，通胀仍居高不下

The U.S. economy experienced a deceleration in growth during the second quarter of 2026, with the advance estimate for Q2 GDP showing an annualized increase of 1.5%. This figure fell short of both the previous quarter's 2.1% growth and analysts' expectations. While consumer spending demonstrated resilience, a significant surge in imports, largely attributed to robust investment in artificial intelligence infrastructure, acted as a drag on the headline GDP number. Concurrently, inflation remains a persistent concern, with the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index rising 3.7% year-over-year in June, and the core PCE index increasing by 3.3%, both still above the Federal Reserve's 2% target. This "slow-growth, sticky-inflation" environment is expected to keep the Fed cautious.

总结：美国经济在2026年第二季度增长放缓，GDP初值年化增长率为1.5%，低于预期。尽管消费者支出表现强劲，但人工智能投资带动的进口激增拖累了整体增长。通胀压力依然存在，6月份PCE价格指数同比上涨3.7%，核心PCE上涨3.3%，均高于美联储2%的目标，预示美联储将保持谨慎立场。

Global Bond Market — 全球债券收益率受政府借贷压力影响

The global bond market continues to face upward pressure on long-term yields, primarily driven by increased government borrowing and persistent budget deficits across major economies. While higher yields are creating new opportunities for investors, the desynchronized monetary policies of central banks worldwide are contributing to a complex fixed-income landscape. The U.S. bond market, in particular, is seeing long-term yields remain elevated even as the Federal Reserve adopts a cautious approach to rate adjustments. Investors are increasingly focusing on these long-term fiscal factors rather than solely on short-term central bank expectations, suggesting that income generation from bonds will be a more significant driver of returns than price appreciation in the latter half of 2026.

总结：全球债券市场长期收益率持续面临上行压力，主要源于各国政府借贷增加和财政赤字。尽管高收益率带来新的投资机会，但全球央行货币政策的不同步性使固定收益市场复杂化。美国债券市场长期收益率居高不下，投资者日益关注长期财政因素，预计2026年下半年债券收益将主要来自利息而非价格上涨。
