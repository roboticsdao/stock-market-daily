investingLive — Why Treasury yields move together but become more volatile further out the curve

Treasury yields across the curve are primarily driven by expectations for the future path of Federal Reserve monetary policy. The front end, such as the 2-year Treasury yield, is closely tied to near-term rate expectations and moves forward-looking before the Fed alters its policy rate. The long end, including 10-year and 30-year yields, is also driven by monetary policy expectations but is more sensitive to economic growth, inflation expectations, and the term premium required for holding long-duration bonds. Stronger economic data like a US PMI report can raise growth expectations, potentially lifting long-term yields by more than the 2-year yield and leading to a bear steepening of the curve.

总结：整条收益率曲线上的国债收益率主要由对美联储货币政策未来路径的预期驱动。 前端收益率（如2年期国债收益率）与近期利率预期紧密相连，并在美联储调整政策利率之前超前变动。 长期端（包括10年期和30年期收益率）同样受货币政策预期驱动，但对经济增长、通胀预期以及持有长期债券所需的期限溢价更为敏感。 更强的经济数据（如美国PMI报告）能够提升增长预期，可能导致长期收益率的升幅超过2年期收益率，从而引发收益率曲线的熊市陡峭化。
