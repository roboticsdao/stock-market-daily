The Motley Fool — New FT Poll Shows: The Future of AI Could Be Good News for International Stocks

A Financial Times survey of economists published on October 17 indicates that 54% of economists believe artificial intelligence will deliver significant gains in economic growth and productivity over the next 10 years, benefiting many or nearly all G20 nations rather than just the U. S. and China. The survey suggests AI will not cause large-scale job losses, as labor-saving productivity gains can help global economies cope with aging populations and shrinking workforces. Echoing this sentiment, Vanguard research projects that the biggest productivity and profit gains from AI will go to companies using the technology rather than those building it, leading to a forecast that international stocks, or global ex-U. S. equities unhedged, will outperform U. S. stocks over the next 30 years. To capture this international growth, the Vanguard Total International Stock ETF holds 8,901 stocks across 43 countries, having delivered annualized returns of about 9.2% over the past 10 years and about 9.4% over the past five years, compared to the S&P 500 index long-term average of 9.98%.

总结：《金融时报》于10月17日发布的一项经济学家调查显示，54%的经济学家认为人工智能将在未来10年为经济增长和生产力带来显著提升，且这些收益将惠及G20的许多或几乎所有国家，而不仅限于美国和中国。 调查表明，AI不会造成大规模失业，因为节省劳动力带来的生产力提升可以帮助全球经济应对人口老龄化和劳动力萎缩的问题。 先锋集团（Vanguard）的研究呼应了这一观点，预测AI带来的最大生产力和利润增长将流向使用该技术的公司，而不是构建该技术的公司，从而预测国际股票（即未对冲的全球除美股票）在未来30年内将跑赢美股。 为了捕捉这种国际增长，先锋富时全球股票ETF（Vanguard Total International Stock ETF）持有了来自43个国家的8,901只股票，其过去10年的年化回报率约为9.2%，过去五年的年化回报率约为9.4%，相比之下标普500指数的长期平均回报率为9.98%。

investingLive — Why Treasury yields move together but become more volatile further out the curve

Treasury yields across the curve are primarily driven by expectations for the future path of Federal Reserve monetary policy. The front end is directly tied to near-term rate expectations, where the 2-year yield reacts to anticipated rate adjustments. Long-term yields, such as the 10-year and 30-year maturities, are also influenced by monetary policy but are more sensitive to growth, inflation expectations, and a term premium that compensates investors for bearing duration risk. Stronger economic growth or persistent inflation can push long-term yields up by more than short-term yields, leading to a bear steepening of the curve.

总结：收益率曲线上的美国国债收益率主要由对美联储货币政策未来路径的预期驱动。 前端与近期利率预期直接相关，其中2年期收益率对预期的利率调整做出反应。 长期收益率（如10年期和30年期）同样受货币政策影响，但对经济增长、通胀预期以及补偿投资者承担久期风险的期限溢价更为敏感。 更强劲的经济增长或持续的通胀可能会推高长期收益率，其涨幅超过短期收益率，从而导致收益率曲线出现熊市陡峭化。
