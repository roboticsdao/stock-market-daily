investingLive — Why Treasury yields move together but become more volatile further out the curve

Treasury yields across the curve are primarily driven by expectations for the future path of Federal Reserve monetary policy. The 2-year yield is directly tied to near-term rate expectations, while the 10-year and 30-year yields are also sensitive to growth and inflation expectations as well as a term premium. Consequently, yields across different maturities tend to move together, but changes become larger and more volatile further out the curve. Stronger economic data, such as a US PMI report, can push long-term yields up faster than short-term yields if investors expect higher long-term interest rates.

总结：收益率曲线上的美国国债收益率主要由对美联储货币政策未来路径的预期驱动。 2年期收益率直接与近期利率预期挂钩，而10年期和30年期收益率则对增长与通胀预期以及期限溢价更为敏感。 因此，不同期限的收益率倾向于同向变动，但越往收益率曲线远端，变动幅度和波动性就越大。 如果投资者预期长期利率走高，更强的经济数据（如美国PMI报告）会推动长期收益率的上升速度快于短期收益率。
