OddsShopper — Kalshi 15-Minute Markets: How a Window Settles

English：Kalshi's 15-minute markets are yes/no contracts operating around the clock for crypto and during stock market sessions for indexes, allowing traders to bet on whether an asset will finish a quarter-hour window at or above its opening price. Market makers reprice contracts instantly off live indices, with participants paying a spread of usually a cent or two per trade. Windows settle based on the average price over the 60 seconds before the window opens compared to the 60 seconds before it closes. Crypto markets feature Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Solana, and Coin Race, while recent expansions added gold, silver, WTI crude, the S&P 500, and the Nasdaq 100. Testing of hourly and 15-minute books revealed fair pricing where market makers capture the edge through the spread.

总结：Kalshi的15分钟市场提供是/否（yes/no）合约，加密货币全天候运行，指数则在股市交易时段运行，允许交易者押注资产在15分钟窗口结束时的价格是否达到或高于开盘价。 做市商根据实时指数即时重新定价合约，参与者每笔交易需支付通常为一美分或两美分的价差。 窗口结算基于开盘前60秒的平均价格与收盘前60秒的平均价格进行对比。 加密货币市场涵盖比特币、以太坊、XRP、Solana及Coin Race，近期扩容还加入了黄金、白银、WTI原油、标普500指数和纳斯达克100指数。 对小时和15分钟订单簿的测试显示出公平定价，做市商通过价差获取优势。
