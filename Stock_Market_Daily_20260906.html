OddsShopper — Kalshi 15-Minute Markets: How a Window Settles

English：Kalshi's 15-minute markets consist of yes/no contracts where participants wager on whether assets like Bitcoin, gold, or stock indexes will finish the next quarter-hour at or above a stamped opening price. Operating continuously for crypto and during regular market hours for indexes and metals, these windows function essentially as fair coin flips where market makers price contracts off live underlying indices and capture profits through a one-to-two-cent spread. Because every window's target price is reset to the current market price at the start, previous streaks and market drifts offer no predictive advantage for future outcomes. Testing over two weeks of live orders revealed no repeatable trading edge, meaning participants should treat these short-duration contracts primarily as entertainment with known admission costs determined by the spread.

总结：Kalshi的15分钟市场包含是/否合约，参与者可押注比特币、黄金或股票指数等资产在下一个15分钟结束时是否会达到或超过盖印的开盘价。 这些时间窗口全天候为加密货币运行，并在常规市场时间内为指数和金属运行，本质上类似于公平的抛硬币，做市商根据实时标的指数为合约定价，并通过一到两美分的价差获取利润。 由于每个窗口的目标价格在开始时都会重置为当前市场价格，因此先前的连胜记录和市场漂移无法为未来结果提供任何预测优势。 经过两周的实盘订单测试，没有发现可重复的交易优势，这意味着参与者应主要将这些短期合约视为娱乐，其已知的入场成本由价差决定。
