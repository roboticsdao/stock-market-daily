The Motley Fool — Why Investing in S&P 500 Index Funds Is a Whole Lot Riskier These Days

The S&P 500 index has historically averaged an annual return of around 10% over the long run, making popular exchange-traded funds like the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF, which features a low expense ratio of 0.03%, attractive for long-term investors. However, investing in S&P 500 index funds has become riskier due to high exposure to the tech sector. All top 10 holdings in the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF have exposure to artificial intelligence, with Nvidia, Apple, and Microsoft accounting for around 22% of the portfolio, and the largest 10 holdings making up nearly 40%. This heavy concentration creates vulnerability to near-term corrections amid growing concerns that an artificial intelligence bubble may have formed. A prolonged market downturn could require several years for recovery, prompting some investors to consider alternative exchange-traded funds that track dividend stocks or defensive sectors to reduce potential declines.

总结：标普500指数在长期内平均年回报率约为10%，使得先锋标普500 ETF等费用率仅为0.03%的流行交易所交易基金成为长期投资者的理想选择。 然而，由于对科技板块的高度敞口，如今投资标普500指数基金变得更加具有风险。 先锋标普500 ETF的前10大持仓全部涉足人工智能，其中英伟达、苹果和微软约占投资组合的22%，而前10大持仓的占比则接近40%。 这种高度集中的科技敞口使得该基金在人们日益担忧人工智能泡沫已经形成的情况下，容易受到短期回调的影响。 如果市场出现长期低迷，可能需要数年时间才能恢复，这促使一些投资者考虑转向追踪股息股或防御性板块的交易所交易基金，以减少潜在的下跌风险。
