English：Value investors frequently use the price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio to uncover bargain stocks, though it has limitations such as excluding debt, failing to evaluate loss-making businesses, and vulnerability to accounting manipulation. Alternatively, the EV-to-EBITDA ratio, also known as the enterprise multiple, offers a comprehensive valuation by dividing a company's enterprise value (market capitalization plus debt and preferred stock minus cash and equivalents) by its earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization. This metric accounts for debt and is useful for evaluating leveraged, acquisition-target, or loss-making yet EBITDA-positive companies, though it cannot be used across different industries due to diverse capital expenditure requirements. Stocks such as Par Pacific Holdings (PARR), Amkor Technology (AMKR), Kohl's Corporation (KSS), Avnet (AVT), and Ecovyst (ECVT) exhibit attractive EV-to-EBITDA multiples. Specific screening parameters to identify bargain stocks include an EV-to-EBITDA ratio and forward P/E below the industry median, price-to-book and price-to-sales ratios below the industry median, estimated one-year EPS growth greater than or equal to the industry median, average 20-day trading volume of at least 100,000, current price of $5 or higher, Zacks Rank of 2 or better, and a Value Score of B or better.

总结：价值投资者经常使用市盈率（P/E）来寻找便宜股票，尽管它存在不包含债务、无法评估亏损企业以及易受会计操纵等局限性。 相比之下，企业价值与息税折旧摊销前利润比率（EV-to-EBITDA），即企业倍数，通过将公司的企业价值（市值加上债务和优先股减去现金及现金等价物）除以息税折旧摊销前利润，提供了更全面的估值。 该指标考虑了债务因素，适用于评估杠杆率高、作为收购目标或亏损但EBITDA为正的公司，不过由于资本支出需求不同，它不能用于不同行业之间的比较。 Par Pacific Holdings (PARR)、Amkor Technology (AMKR)、Kohl's Corporation (KSS)、Avnet (AVT)和Ecovyst (ECVT)等股票展现出具有吸引力的EV-to-EBITDA倍数。 筛选便宜股票的具体参数包括：12个月最近的EV-to-EBITDA以及前瞻市盈率低于行业中位数，市净率和市销率低于行业中位数，预计一年期每股收益增长率大于或等于行业中位数，20日平均成交量不低于10万，当前价格不低于5美元， Zacks评级为2或更好，价值评分为B或更好。
