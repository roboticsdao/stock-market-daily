After months of heavy selling and significant outflows, ETF demand has finally turned positive again, with rolling 30-day flows now above 20,000 BTC. This institutional support is crucial, suggesting that the recent stabilization around the $60,000 level is now backed by genuine demand rather than mere optimism. While the broader bear market drawdown remains, the shift in ETF flows indicates a healthier market structure, with a short-term outlook pointing towards continued stabilization between $58,000 and $68,000.

总结：2026年8月初，比特币市场出现企稳迹象，主要得益于美国现货比特币ETF资金流入的积极转变。在经历了数月的抛售和大量资金外流后，ETF需求终于再次转为正值，过去30天的滚动流入量已超过20,000枚比特币。这种机构支持至关重要，表明比特币在60,000美元附近的近期稳定现在得到了真实需求的支撑，而非仅仅是乐观情绪。尽管整体熊市回调仍在，但ETF资金流向的变化预示着更健康的市场结构，短期展望指向在58,000美元至68,000美元之间持续稳定。
