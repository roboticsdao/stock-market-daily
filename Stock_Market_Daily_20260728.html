U.S. Trade Representative — 美国贸易代表办公室对60个经济体征收新关税

The Office of the U.S. Trade Representative (USTR) has imposed new Section 301 tariffs, ranging from 10% to 12.5%, against imports from 60 economies. These tariffs target alleged forced labor practices and became effective for goods entered for consumption or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption before 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on July 28, 2026. This move replaces temporary Section 122 tariffs and follows a sweeping global investigation, signaling a continued aggressive stance on trade policy and potentially impacting global supply chains and prices. Companies with exposure to these economies are advised to assess their duty exposure immediately.

总结：美国贸易代表办公室（USTR）已对来自60个经济体的进口商品征收新的301条款关税，税率介于10%至12.5%之间。这些关税旨在打击所谓的强迫劳动行为，并于2026年7月28日美国东部时间凌晨12:01前生效。此举取代了临时性的122条款关税，并是在一项全球调查之后实施的，预示着美国贸易政策的持续强硬立场，可能影响全球供应链和物价。
