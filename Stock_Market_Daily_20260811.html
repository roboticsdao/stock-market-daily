US Economy — 美国第二季度GDP增长放缓，通胀压力依然存在

The U.S. economy expanded at a slower pace in the second quarter of 2026, with real gross domestic product (GDP) increasing at an annual rate of 1.5%, down from 2.1% in the first quarter.
