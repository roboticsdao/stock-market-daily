qz.com — Magnificent Seven stocks and their share of the S&P 500

As of December 2025, seven large U. S. technology companies known as the Magnificent Seven held close to 35% of the S&P 500's market value, according to Vantage Markets. The group includes Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Nvidia, Meta Platforms, and Tesla. Spanning semiconductors, cloud computing, e-commerce, social media, and electric vehicles, these companies share a reliance on digital ecosystems, cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and data, causing them to move in similar directions during economic or technology shifts. Because the S&P 500 weights each member by market capitalization, these large members exert greater influence on the index and create a concentrated portfolio for index funds and passive strategies. In 2025, the seven accounted for 42.5% of the index's 17.9% total return, marking the first year since 2021 that they delivered less than half of the total return.

总结：根据Vantage Markets的数据，截至2025年12月，被称为“七巨头”的七家大型美国科技公司占标普500指数市值近35%。 该集团包括苹果、微软、亚马逊、Alphabet、英伟达、Meta Platforms和特斯拉。 这些公司跨越半导体、云计算、电子商务、社交媒体和电动汽车领域，共同依赖数字生态系统、云基础设施、人工智能和数据，这使得它们在经济或技术发生转变时往往朝着相同的方向变动。 由于标普500指数按市值对每个成员进行加权，这些大型成员对指数施加了更大的影响力，并为指数基金和被动策略创造了集中的投资组合。 在2025年，这七家公司占该指数17.9%总回报率的42.5%，标志着自2021年以来它们贡献的总回报率首次低于一半。
