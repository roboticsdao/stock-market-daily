OddsShopper — Kalshi 15-Minute Markets: How a Window Settles

English：Kalshi introduced 15-minute event-contract markets spanning crypto, gold, silver, WTI crude, the S&P 500, and the Nasdaq 100. These contracts resolve based on whether the simple average of a reference feed over the final 60 seconds before a window closes is at or above the stamped open-average price. Trading costs are primarily driven by the bid-ask spread, which acts as the exchange's built-in edge since every window starts as a fresh coin flip.

总结：Kalshi推出了涵盖加密货币、黄金、白银、WTI原油、标普500指数和纳斯达克100指数的15分钟事件合约市场。 这些合约的结算依据是窗口关闭前最后60秒参考源的简单平均值是否等于或高于盖章的开盘平均价格。 交易成本主要由买卖价差驱动，由于每个窗口都是一次全新的硬币抛掷，这构成了交易所内置的优势。
