OddsShopper — Kalshi 15-Minute Markets: How a Window Settles

English：Kalshi has introduced 15-minute event contracts, featuring one question per window determining whether a price will finish the next quarter hour at or above a stamped target, operating around the clock across cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and XRP, as well as gold, silver, WTI crude, the S&P 500, and the Nasdaq 100. These CFTC-regulated binary contracts translate exchange prices directly into market-implied probabilities, while the bid-ask spread functions as the exchange's house edge. Settlement is determined by comparing the open-average target against the simple average of the 60-second reference price feed immediately preceding the window's close, meaning every window starts as a fresh coin flip.

总结：Kalshi推出了15分钟事件合约，每个窗口包含一个问题，用来决定价格在接下来的15分钟结束时是否会达到或超过既定目标，全天候在比特币、以太坊和瑞波币等加密货币以及黄金、白银、WTI原油、标普500和纳斯达克100指数上运行。 这些受CFTC监管的二元合约将交易所价格直接转化为市场内含概率，而买卖价差则充当了交易所的抽头优势。 结算标准是将开盘平均目标价与窗口关闭前即时60秒参考价格走势的简单平均值进行对比，这意味着每个窗口都从一次全新的抛硬币开始。
