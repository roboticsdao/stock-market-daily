Since 1928, the S&P 500 has averaged a 1. 1% decline in September with a negative finish roughly 56% of the time, while the Nasdaq Composite has averaged a 0. 9% drop since 1971, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average has fallen 1. 1% on average since 1897 with a positive finish only 42% of the time. Artificial intelligence chip stocks, including Nvidia, Broadcom, and Advanced Micro Devices, have experienced varying performances during recent September periods. In September 2023, sector ETFs like the VanEck Semiconductor ETF and the iShares Semiconductor ETF dropped about 7%, underperforming in some single-stock comparisons, whereas both ETFs were flat in September 2024 and gained 12% and 11% respectively in September 2025. Despite historical seasonal patterns, strong earnings, falling interest rates, and continuous demand for AI data center infrastructure and memory can override the September Effect, though growth-oriented chip stocks with high valuations remain susceptible to immediate sell-offs during periods of market unease.

总结：被称为“九月效应”的季节性市场异常现象历来在劳动节前后给股市带来逆风，因为基金经理们会重新平衡投资组合并进行税损收割。 自1928年以来，标普500指数在9月份的平均跌幅为1. 1%，约有56%的时间以下跌收场；纳斯达克综合指数自1971年以来的平均跌幅为0. 9%；道琼斯工业平均指数自1897年以来在9月份平均下跌1. 1%，只有42%的时间录得正收益。 包括英伟达、博通和超威半导体在内的人工智能芯片股票在近几年的9月份表现各异。 在2023年9月，VanEck半导体ETF和安硕半导体ETF等行业ETF下跌约7%，在某些单只股票的对比中表现落后；而在2024年9月，这两只ETF均持平；在2025年9月，它们分别上涨了12%和11%。 尽管存在历史季节性规律，但强劲的收益、不断下降的利率以及对AI数据中心基础设施和内存的持续需求可以压倒九月效应，尽管估值高昂的成长型芯片股在市场不安时期仍然容易遭到抛售。
