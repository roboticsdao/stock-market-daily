Gold is a scarce, durable precious metal valued for thousands of years across human civilizations for jewelry, art, coinage, and industrial, medical, and electronics applications. Formed in massive stars and scattered by supernovae, gold was incorporated into Earth during its formation about 4.5 billion years ago, with the majority sinking to the core while some remained in the crust and mantle. According to the World Gold Council, total mined gold historically amounts to approximately 200,000 metric tons. The metal possesses high market liquidity, operating 24 hours a day, seven days a week across major global trading hubs such as London, New York, and Shanghai.
