Tyson Foods, Inc. (NYSE: TSN) announced its third quarter fiscal year 2026 results today, reporting sales of $13,868 million, which remained flat compared to the prior year. Excluding the impact of a legal contingency accrual, sales were up 0.6%. The company delivered GAAP operating income of $362 million, a 39% increase from the prior year, and adjusted operating income of $547 million, up 8%. GAAP EPS was $0.52, a significant rise of $0.35 from the year-ago quarter, while adjusted EPS increased 9% to $0.99. The strong performance was primarily fueled by continued strength in its Chicken and Prepared Foods segments.

总结：泰森食品（Tyson Foods, Inc.）今日公布了2026财年第三季度业绩，销售额为138.68亿美元，与去年同期持平。剔除法律或有事项拨备后，销售额增长0.6%。公司GAAP营业收入为3.62亿美元，同比增长39%；调整后营业收入为5.47亿美元，增长8%。GAAP每股收益为0.52美元，调整后每股收益增长9%至0.99美元，主要得益于鸡肉和预制食品业务的强劲表现。
