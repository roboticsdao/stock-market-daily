Bitcoin (BTC) saw a slight dip of 0.4% to trade around $63,384 today, influenced by a $144.6 million net outflow from US spot Bitcoin ETFs on August 10, marking the first reversal after five days of inflows. Additionally, significant BTC sales by Strategy (MSTR) added to the selling pressure. Despite these headwinds, institutional demand showed signs of revival earlier in the month, and on-chain metrics suggest long-term holders are absorbing losses, consistent with a market bottom. The cryptocurrency remains range-bound, balancing cautious institutional flows with broader macroeconomic uncertainties and awaiting a key SEC regulatory vote on its 'Regulation Crypto' framework scheduled for August 14, 2026.

总结：今日比特币（BTC）小幅下跌0.4%至63,384美元附近，主要受8月10日美国现货比特币ETF净流出1.446亿美元的影响，这是连续五天流入后的首次逆转。此外，Strategy（MSTR）的大量比特币出售也增加了抛售压力。尽管面临这些不利因素，机构需求在本月初显示出复苏迹象，链上指标表明长期持有者正在吸收损失，这与市场底部相符。

Global Economy — 全球继续投资美国的原因

Despite recent global turbulence, the United States continues to attract a significant share of worldwide investment, with its financial system maintaining a dominant gravitational pull. The U.S. has captured an increasing portion of global direct investment, rising from 15% pre-pandemic to an average of 20% since 2024. The U.S. equity market now accounts for nearly 65% of global market capitalization, and the dollar remains near its 40-year high, with approximately 90% of central bank transactions still dollar-based. This sustained investment is attributed to America's robust financial institutions, competitive banking sector, and a strong willingness by investors to finance future economic growth, particularly in areas like AI infrastructure and energy capabilities.

总结：尽管全球近期动荡，美国金融体系的强大吸引力使其继续吸引全球大部分投资。自2024年以来，美国在全球直接投资中的份额已从疫情前的15%增至平均20%。目前，美国股市占全球市值的近65%，美元指数也接近40年高点，约90%的央行交易仍以美元计价。这种持续的投资得益于美国健全的金融机构、竞争激烈的银行业以及投资者对未来经济增长（尤其是在人工智能基础设施和能源领域）的强烈投资意愿。
