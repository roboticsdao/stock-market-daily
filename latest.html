OddsShopper — Kalshi 15-Minute Markets: How a Window Settles

English：Kalshi's 15-minute markets feature yes/no contracts predicting whether assets like Bitcoin or gold will finish the next quarter hour at or above an opening stamped price, with a new window starting every 15 minutes. Crypto markets run continuously, while metals and index windows align with underlying market hours. Market makers reprice based on live indices, creating fair coin-flip odds where the spread represents the primary cost, typically one or two cents. Testing over two weeks revealed no repeatable entry beating these fair prices, indicating that the edge belongs entirely to the market maker's spread.

总结：Kalshi 的 15 分钟市场提供是非合约，预测比特币或黄金等资产在下一个 15 分钟结束时是否会达到或超过开盘时的盖印价格，每 15 分钟开启一个新的时间窗口。 加密货币市场持续运行，而金属和指数窗口则与底层市场时间保持一致。 做市商根据实时指数重新定价，创造出公平的硬币翻转几率，其中价差代表主要成本，通常为一到两分钱。 经过两周的测试，没有发现任何能够击败这些公允价格的可重复入场方式，这表明优势完全属于做市商的价差。
