Livemint — S&P 500 vs Nasdaq 100: Which global index fund is right for Indian investors seeking international market exposure?

Indian investors seeking international exposure can consider the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 indices to diversify beyond domestic mutual funds. According to Akshay Falgunia, Fund Manager at PPFAS GIFT, the US accounts for over 60% of global stock market value, with the S&P 500 representing more than 40%. The S&P 500 covers about 500 leading US companies across sectors, offering broad international diversification. The Nasdaq 100 includes 100 of the largest non-financial Nasdaq-listed companies with a heavy focus on technology and biotechnology. Falgunia suggests the S&P 500 as a starting point for broad exposure, while the Nasdaq 100 suits those seeking innovation exposure and accepting higher volatility. The report warns against concentrating an entire overseas portfolio in artificial intelligence or a few technology stocks. International investments can help lower geographical concentration, access businesses with limited representation in India, and fund future foreign-currency expenses. Beginners can consider allocating around 10% of their equity portfolio overseas, which can later increase to 25% to 33% for those comfortable with international markets. Falgunia recommends starting with broad-based passive funds and ETFs before moving to individual stocks or concentrated themes, while noting that global indices like the MSCI All Country World Index offer even wider geographical diversification.

总结：寻求国际市场敞口的印度投资者可以考虑标普500指数和纳斯达克100指数，以实现超越国内共同基金的多元化配置。 根据PPFAS GIFT基金经理Akshay Falgunia的说法，美国占全球股市价值的60%以上，仅标普500指数代表的公司就占全球股市价值的40%以上。 标普500指数包含约500家跨多个行业的美国领先企业，提供广泛的国际多元化选择。 纳斯达克100指数则包含纳斯达克交易所上市的100家最大非金融公司，在科技和生物技术领域集中度更高。 Falgunia将标普500指数视为寻求广泛国际敞口投资者的起点，而对于寻求更多创新敞口且能承受更高波动的投资者，他则推荐纳斯达克100指数。 该报告警告称，不应将全部海外投资组合集中在人工智能或其他热门投资主题上。 国际投资有助于降低地理集中度，并为未来海外教育或旅游等外汇支出做准备。 初次进行国际投资的投资者可以考虑将其股票投资总额的约10%配置到海外投资中，随着对国际市场的熟悉，可逐步增加至25%到33%。 Falgunia建议从广泛的被动型基金和ETF开始，再逐步转向个股或集中的投资主题。
