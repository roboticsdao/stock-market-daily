investingLive — How could Japan shake up the global markets?

Minutes from the Bank of Japan’s October meeting point to a potential rate hike in December, driven by a weak yen and rising energy costs from Middle East tensions that pushed core inflation to 2.1% year over year in September, exceeding the 2% target, while the measure excluding food and energy reached 2.4%. While tighter policy could control inflation and ease yen pressure, it would increase borrowing costs and exacerbate the burden of Japan’s massive government debt exceeding 200% of GDP. Currency intervention by selling US dollars and buying yen could also curb imported inflation, but Japan’s reserves are limited and largely held in US Treasuries, meaning such sales could raise Treasury yields and pressure global markets. Additionally, if the Federal Reserve moves in the opposite direction and the carry trade unwinds, investors who borrowed yen to buy foreign assets might be forced to sell them, potentially repeating the 2024 market sell-off.

总结：日本央行10月会议纪要显示12月可能再次加息，原因在于日元疲软以及中东局势紧张推高了能源成本，导致9月份核心通胀率同比达到2.1%，超过了日本央行2%的目标，而剔除食品和能源的指标则攀升至2.4%。 虽然收紧政策有助于控制通胀并缓解日元压力，但也会提高借贷成本，并使超过GDP 200%的庞大政府债务的偿还变得更加昂贵。 通过卖出美元买入日元的干预措施也可以遏制输入型通胀，但日本的外汇储备有限且大部分持 有美国国债，抛售这些债券可能会推高美债收益率并对全球市场施加压力。 此外，如果美联储采取相反的行动并且日元套利交易解体，借入日元购买海外资产的投资者可能被迫抛售资产，从而可能引发类似2024年的市场抛售重演。
