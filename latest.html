English：Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is planning to partner with major Wall Street firms, including Goldman Sachs, BlackRock, Blackstone, KKR, Apollo, and Brookfield, to potentially raise $500 billion through chip-backed securities where graphics processing units serve as collateral.
