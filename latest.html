Renewed buying in semiconductor and artificial intelligence-related shares drove the recovery, aided by rising U. S. Nasdaq futures. Advantest climbed 7.06% to 26,585 yen, Tokyo Electron advanced 4.43% to 8,220 yen, and SoftBank Group gained 3.12% to 2,984.5 yen. Prime Market turnover reached approximately 7.79 trillion yen on volume of 2.40 billion shares, with 926 advancing issues and 582 decliners.

总结：在11月27日周五，日本股市大幅反弹，日经225指数收盘上涨1,212日元或2.11%，报58,711日元，东证指数上涨64.80点或1.73%，报3,815.20点。 半导体和人工智能相关股票的重新买入推动了基准指数从上周的跌势中复苏，午盘涨幅因美国纳斯达克期货上涨而扩大。 Advantest上涨7.06%至26,585日元，东京电子上涨4.43%至8,220日元，软银集团上涨3.12%至2,984.5日元。 东交所主板成交额达到约7.79 trillion，成交量为24.0亿股，上涨股票926只，下跌股票582只。

TradingPedia — GBP Rises vs Yen as Markets Look Past Hot Tokyo CPI

The British Pound traded nearly 0.3% higher near 209.20 against the Japanese Yen during Friday's European session as the Yen lagged. Tokyo core CPI excluding fresh food rose 2.9% year-on-year in November, accelerating from 2.6% in October and reaching its fastest pace since July 2025, according to Danske Bank. While the increase was partly driven by the end of government utility subsidies, prices rose across a broad range of goods and services. The data supports expectations for a Bank of Japan rate hike at its December 17-18 meeting. Meanwhile, BoJ board member Naoki Tamura warned of inflation overshoot risks, and markets fully expect the Bank of England to deliver a 25 basis point rate hike to 4.25% on December 17.
