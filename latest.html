24/7 Wall St. — Smuggling Nvidia Chips Into China Is About to Become a Crime in Taiwan

On October 16, 2026, Taiwan's Minister of Economic Affairs Kung Ming-hsin confirmed the plan, though he gave no timeline or start date, and a legal amendment is still required. The proposed measures correspond with U. S. controls and follow a January 15 trade agreement that cut U. S. tariffs on most Taiwanese goods from 20% to 15% alongside $250 billion in Taiwanese company commitments for U. S. projects. DPP legislator Chung Chia-pin has also drafted an amendment adding a mainland China semiconductor clause that must pass the legislature. Nvidia has excluded China data center compute revenue from its third-quarter fiscal 2027 outlook of $108.0 billion. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing raised its fourth-quarter revenue guidance from $52.3 billion to $54.3 billion. Super Micro Computer builds systems in Taiwan and faces an independent review of export-control-related transactions, making it the most exposed of the three companies.

总结：台湾宣布计划将人工智能服务器和尖端半导体纳入其管制货物清单，根据《对外贸易法》，这将使向中国大陆运送这些物品的行为构成犯罪。 2026年10月16日，台湾经济部门负责人郭智辉（Kung Ming-hsin）证实了这一计划，但他没有给出具体时间表或开始日期，该计划仍需通过法律修正案。 这些拟议的措施与美国的管制相对应，并遵循了1月15日签署的贸易协定，该协定将美国对大多数台湾商品的关税从20%降至15%，并伴随台湾企业对美国项目的2500亿美元承诺。 民进党民意代表钟佳滨也起草了一项增加中国大陆半导体条款的修正案，尚待通过。 英伟达在其1080亿美元的2027财年第三季度展望中已排除了所有中国数据中心计算收入。 台积电将第四季度营收指引从523亿美元上调至543亿美元。 超微电脑在台湾制造系统，其董事会已披露了一项针对出口管制问题相关交易的独立审查，这使其成为三家公司中面临风险最大、波动性最高的一家。
