TechBullion — How Interest Rate Expectations Move the US Dollar

Foreign exchange markets continuously price future interest rate expectations, meaning currency movements often occur before official central bank announcements. The US dollar responds to shifts in economic indicators such as inflation reports, employment data, and Treasury yields, particularly the two-year yield. Factors such as core inflation, labor market strength, and relative rate outlooks against other central banks like the eurozone dictate investor positioning and currency valuations.

总结：外汇市场持续对未来的利率预期进行定价，这意味着汇率波动往往发生在官方央行宣布决定之前。 美元对通胀报告、就业数据和美国国债收益率（尤其是两年期收益率）等经济指标的变化作出反应。 核心inflation、劳动力市场强度以及相对于欧洲央行等其他央行的相对利率前景，共同决定了投资者的持仓和货币估值。
