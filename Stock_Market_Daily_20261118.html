In London, the Competition Appeal Tribunal completely dismissed a 2 billion pound, equivalent to 26 billion dollar, lawsuit brought against Sony over allegations that digital game prices for the PlayStation console were inflated due to the company's dominant position. The claimants argued that Sony abused its dominant position by restricting digital game purchases and add-on content to the PlayStation Store. Sony explained during the proceedings that it invested years and billions in an integrated platform beneficial to consumers in a competitive market, noting that competitors like Nintendo and Microsoft’s Xbox use similar business models. The Tribunal ruled that the claimants failed to prove Sony violated competition law. Stephanie Burns, Senior Vice President and General Counsel for Sony Interactive Entertainment, stated the ruling validates the company's business model.

总结：在伦敦，竞争审判所全面驳回了一起针对索尼的20亿英镑（约合26亿美元）规模诉讼，原告指控索尼的“PlayStation”数字游戏价格因其垄断地位而被哄抬。 原告主张索尼将游戏机数字游戏及追加内容的买卖限定在“PlayStation Store”属于滥用支配地位。 索尼在审理中解释称，公司在竞争市场中为消费者带来利益的一体化游戏平台上投入了多年的岁月和数十亿规模的资金，并指出竞争对手任天堂和微软的“Xbox”也采用了相似的业务模式。 英国竞争审判所判定原告未能证实索尼违反竞争法。 索尼互动娱乐高级副总裁兼总法律顾问斯蒂芬妮·伯恩斯表示，此次裁决证实了该公司业务模式的正当性。
