OddsShopper — Kalshi 15-Minute Markets: How a Window Settles

English：Kalshi's 15-minute markets are CFTC-regulated event contracts where users bet yes or no on whether Bitcoin, gold, or indices will finish a 15-minute window at or above a price stamped at the open. Windows run continuously for crypto and follow underlying market hours for metals and indexes. The target price is calculated as the average price over the 60 seconds before the window opens and is compared to the same 60-second average at the close. Market makers reprice off live indices, meaning prices function near fair coin flips, leaving the spread as the primary cost of trading.

总结：Kalshi的15分钟市场是受美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）监管的事件合约，用户可对比特币、黄金或指数在15分钟时间窗口结束时的价格是否达到或超过开盘时设定的价格进行“是”或“否”的押注。 加密货币的窗口全天候持续运行，而金属和指数则遵循底层市场的交易时间。 目标价格计算为窗口开启前60秒内的平均价格，并与收盘时的同一60秒平均值进行比较。 做市商根据实时指数重新定价，这意味着价格接近公平硬币抛掷，交易的主要成本在于买卖价差。
