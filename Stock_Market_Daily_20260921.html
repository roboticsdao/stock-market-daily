English：Global bond markets are experiencing a structural repricing as governments issue more debt amid persistent inflation, geopolitical conflicts, and decreasing investor demand, pushing major sovereign bond yields toward multi-decade highs. The 10-year U. S. Treasury yield climbed to approximately 5% in mid-September, while the 30-year yield reached its highest level since 2008. In response to inflation, the U. S. Federal Reserve raised its base interest rate by 0. 25 percentage points to 4%. Japan's 10-year bond yield moved above 3% for the first time since 1996 following discussions involving U. S. and Japanese officials. In the UK, the 10-year gilt yield neared 5. 3%, and the European Central Bank raised its three key rates by 25 basis points on September 10, 2026. These rising yields are driven by increased government borrowing, geopolitical fragmentation, and energy market disruptions, with Brent crude surpassing $108 per barrel due to Middle East conflicts.

总结：由于各国政府在持续的通胀、地缘政治冲突以及投资者需求减少的背景下发行更多债务，全球债券市场正在经历结构性重新定价，主要的主权债券收益率被推至数十年来的高位。 9月中旬，美国10年期国债收益率攀升至约5%，而30年期收益率则达到了2008年以来的最高水平。 为了应对通胀，美联储将其基准利率上调0. 25个百分点至4%。 在美日官员进行讨论后，日本10年期国债收益率自1996年以来首次升至3%以上。 在英国，10年期国债收益率接近5. 3%，欧洲央行也在2026年9月10日将三个主要利率上调了25个基点。 这些不断上升的收益率是由政府借款增加、地缘政治碎片化以及能源市场动荡（由于中东冲突导致布伦特原油突破每桶$108）共同驱动的。
